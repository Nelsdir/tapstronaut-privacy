# TAPSTRONAUT Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 September 2026**

Nels Dir Games ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile game TAPSTRONAUT ("the Game").

## 1. Information We Collect

Nels Dir Games does not require you to create a TAPSTRONAUT account and does not directly collect information such as your name, postal address, telephone number or date of birth through the Game.

The Game does, however, use third-party services that may automatically collect or process certain information when you use it. Depending on your device, settings, location and consent choices, this may include:

- device and app identifiers;
- advertising identifiers;
- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device type, operating system and technical information;
- advertising interaction and performance information;
- gameplay-related information required for platform gaming services and leaderboards; and
- diagnostic, security and fraud-prevention information.

## 2. Advertising

TAPSTRONAUT uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses and information about interactions with advertisements in order to provide, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalise advertising.

The advertising presented to you may depend on your location, age-related settings, device settings and the privacy choices or consent you provide.

For more information about how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Consent and Advertising Choices

Where required by applicable law, including in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland, users may be presented with a consent message before certain advertising-related data processing takes place.

Your available choices may include consenting to or declining certain uses of personal data for advertising purposes.

On Apple devices, Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework may also be used where permission is required for tracking. You may change relevant tracking permissions through your device settings.

You can also manage advertising and privacy choices through the privacy controls provided by your device, Apple, Google and, where available, consent options presented within the Game.

## 4. Leaderboards and Platform Gaming Services

TAPSTRONAUT supports platform gaming and leaderboard functionality.

On Apple devices, these features may use Apple Game Center. On Android devices, they may use Google Play Games Services.

Information associated with these services is processed by Apple or Google in accordance with the privacy settings of your platform account and their respective privacy policies.

TAPSTRONAUT may submit gameplay information, such as scores or completion times, to these services so that leaderboard functionality can operate.

Nels Dir Games does not operate a separate TAPSTRONAUT user account system for these leaderboards.

Apple's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the Game and its third-party services may be used to:

- provide and operate TAPSTRONAUT;
- provide advertising;
- provide rewarded advertising features;
- operate platform leaderboards and gaming services;
- measure advertising performance;
- maintain security and prevent fraud or abuse;
- diagnose technical problems; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 6. Sharing of Information

Nels Dir Games does not sell personal information directly to third parties.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers integrated with the Game, principally Google for advertising and platform services and Apple for platform gaming services on Apple devices.

These third parties process information under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection obligations.

We expect third-party providers with whom user data is shared through the Game to provide appropriate protection for that information in accordance with applicable law.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Nels Dir Games does not maintain a separate TAPSTRONAUT user-account database containing personal profiles.

Information processed by Apple, Google or advertising providers may be retained according to those providers' respective retention policies.

If you wish to request access to or deletion of personal information held directly by Nels Dir Games, or have a privacy-related question, contact us using the details below.

Requests concerning information associated directly with Apple Game Center, Google Play Games or Google advertising services may also need to be managed through the relevant provider's account or privacy controls.

## 8. Children's Privacy

TAPSTRONAUT is not designed to require children to provide personal information directly to Nels Dir Games.

Advertising and data processing will be configured in accordance with applicable platform requirements and laws concerning younger users.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us and we will investigate and, where appropriate, delete the information.

## 9. International Data Processing

Third-party providers used by TAPSTRONAUT may process information in countries other than the country in which you live.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to the safeguards and privacy practices of those providers and applicable data protection law.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information and to object to or restrict certain processing.

You may also be able to withdraw consent for certain processing through the Game's consent interface or through your device or platform privacy settings.

To make a privacy request concerning information held directly by Nels Dir Games, contact us at the email address below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if TAPSTRONAUT's features, third-party services or legal requirements change.
